隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日

本政策适用于 iOS 应用“周易卜卦 Zhouyi”（以下简称“本应用”），开发者为 LS（Louis Wang）。

我们不收集任何数据

本应用不收集、不上传、不出售也不分享任何个人信息或使用数据。本应用不发起任何网络请求，没有账户系统，不包含分析统计、广告或追踪功能。

数据仅保存在本机

你的起卦记录、问题与笔记仅保存在你的设备上，开发者无法访问。你可以在应用内随时删除单条记录；删除本应用后，所有数据也会一并删除。

无第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方 SDK 或服务，也没有内购。

儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会在此页面发布新版本并更新生效日期。

联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请发邮件至 lswang6@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS app “周易卜卦 Zhouyi” (the “App”), developed by LS (Louis Wang).

We collect no data

The App does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It makes no network requests, has no accounts, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your data stays on your device

Your readings, questions and notes are stored only on your device, and the developer cannot access them. You can delete any reading in the App at any time, and deleting the App removes all of its data.

No third parties

The App includes no third-party SDKs or services and has no in-app purchases.

Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
