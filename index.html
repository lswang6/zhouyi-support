周易卜卦 Zhouyi

离线的周易起卦与学习应用 · An offline I Ching app for iPhone

支持

周易卜卦是一款完全离线的周易起卦与学习应用。支持铜钱、数字、时间三种起卦方式，提供卦辞、爻辞等经典原文解读，保存历次起卦记录，并附有易学知识专栏。界面支持 11 种语言。

起卦是怎样进行的？

可选择铜钱起卦（摇六次，每次三枚铜钱）、数字起卦（输入三个数）或时间起卦（以当前时间推算）。得出本卦与变爻后，应用会显示对应的经典原文与解读。

历史记录存在哪里？

所有起卦记录和笔记只保存在你的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。应用不联网，也没有账户。

如何删除记录？

在“记录”页打开某条记录，滑到底部点“删除此记录”并确认即可。删除应用本身也会一并清除全部记录。

卦象结果可以作为决策依据吗？

本应用用于了解传统文化、学习周易与自我反思，不构成任何医疗、法律、财务或其他专业建议。重要决定请咨询相关专业人士。

需要付费或联网吗？

不需要。应用没有内购、广告或订阅，全部功能离线可用。

隐私政策

Support

Zhouyi (周易卜卦) is a fully offline app for I Ching divination and study. It offers three casting methods (coins, numbers and time), hexagram readings with the classical text, a history of your past readings, and a column of articles on I Ching learning. The interface is available in 11 languages.

How does casting work?

Choose coin casting (six tosses of three coins), number casting (enter three numbers) or time casting (derived from the current time). Once the hexagram and any changing lines are determined, the app shows the classical text and its interpretation.

Where is my history stored?

All readings and notes are stored only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. The app does not connect to the internet and has no accounts.

How do I delete a reading?

Open the reading from the History tab, scroll to the bottom, tap “Delete reading” and confirm. Deleting the app also removes all of its records.

Should I rely on a reading to make decisions?

The app is meant for cultural study and personal reflection. It is not medical, legal, financial or any other professional advice. For important decisions, please consult a qualified professional.

Does it cost anything or need the internet?

No. There are no in-app purchases, ads or subscriptions, and every feature works offline.
