周易 · 经传白话 Zhouyi

离线的《周易》经传读本 · An offline reader for the I Ching classic text

支持

周易 · 经传白话是一款完全离线的《周易》研读应用：六十四卦卦辞、爻辞与十翼传文，逐句配现代白话译文；附十二篇易学专栏、今日一卦小组件，以及演示传统起卦方法的古法演示与读易笔记。界面支持 11 种语言。

从哪里开始读？

打开应用即是“易学”页：点“六十四卦”逐卦查阅经传原文与白话，或从专栏文章读起。

“起卦”页是什么？

演示《系辞》所载大衍筮法、铜钱法以及梅花易数的数字、时间起卦，换算步骤逐条列出，帮助理解古人如何由数成卦，并标出依朱熹变占规则应读的经文。应用不提供运势或吉凶预测。

笔记存在哪里？

读易笔记保存在你的设备上；若设备登录 iCloud，会同步到你本人 iCloud 的私有数据库。开发者没有服务器，也无法访问你的数据。

如何删除笔记？

在“笔记”页打开某条笔记，滑到底部点“删除此记录”并确认即可。

需要付费吗？

不需要。应用没有内购、广告或订阅，全部功能离线可用。

隐私政策

Support

Zhouyi: I Ching Classic Text is a fully offline reader and study companion for the Book of Changes: all 64 hexagrams with James Legge’s complete translation of the judgments, line statements and Ten Wings commentaries, twelve illustrated articles, Hexagram of the Day, a walkthrough of the traditional casting methods, and a journal for your notes. The interface is available in 11 languages.

Where do I start?

The app opens on the Learn tab. Tap “The 64 Hexagrams” to read any hexagram’s text and commentaries, or start with the articles.

What is the Cast tab?

It demonstrates the historical methods described in the classic (yarrow stalks, coins, and the Plum Blossom number and time methods), showing every step, and points to the passage that Zhu Xi’s classical rules say to read. The app makes no predictions.

Where is my journal stored?

Journal entries stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. The developer has no server and cannot access your data.

How do I delete an entry?

Open the entry from the Journal tab, scroll to the bottom, tap “Delete reading” and confirm.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no in-app purchases, ads or subscriptions, and every feature works offline.
